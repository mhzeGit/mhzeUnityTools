# Painted Stylized House Style

Use actual game references with these defaults. The user's chosen style takes precedence, including the approved flatter cartoon direction.

## Shared language

- Chunky, gently rounded, mildly imperfect handmade silhouettes.
- Near-black outer contours and selective internal seams.
- Broad painted colour areas, purposeful material strokes and readable tonal groups.
- Moderate detail: richer than primitives, quieter than photoreal PBR. Most surfaces remain quiet; roughly 75% is a heuristic, not a numerical requirement.
- Warm umber/ochre wood, muted charcoal/slate iron, parchment yellows, earthy reds and creams, with restrained contrasting hues where appropriate.
- Keep each material recognizable. Do not apply woodgrain, scratches or the same wear pattern to every item.

## Rendering choice

**Flatter painted cartoon:** confident contours, two or three broad tones, simplified volume and sparse painted texture. Keep thickness and part relationships understandable. Flatness is not a defect when chosen by the user.

**Painted 3D mesh appearance:** broad planar-to-rounded transitions, softened bevels, painted shading around volume and restrained highlights. Use when requested or supported by the selected reference.

Avoid adding photographic microtexture, glossy toy plastic, dense scratch noise or cinematic lighting unless requested.

## Setting and reference roles

Default fiction is mundane handmade medieval/domestic equipment with a faintly unsettling horror-comedy character. Transfer rendering and palette from later-period furnishings without copying their period into a medieval item.

Default reference-sheet presentation is a pale neutral background and restrained lighting without a global orange tint, vignette or bloom. Respect requested transparency, background or mood changes.

Do not add modern branding, unnecessary fantasy decoration, faces or magic by default. These are defaults, not bans on a requested mask, heraldry or characterful object.

Project screenshots establish style. TEMP meshes do not establish final shape. Real object references establish silhouette and component arrangement, not automatically texture or period. Generated examples establish only the aspects the user approved. Never reuse rejected geometry because its painting is attractive.
